## Brand & Style

This design system defines a high-end physical product rental marketplace. The visual architecture unites the rigor of modern Scandinavian minimalism with the organic warmth of an enchanted temperate arboretum. It rejects cluttered transactional e-commerce conventions in favor of a spatial, editorial gallery where high-value physical artifacts—from medium-format cameras and rare acoustic instruments to architectural furniture and outdoor expedition gear—are presented with museum-grade curation.

The core design movement is **Bento Spatial Minimalism**:
- **Bento Hierarchy**: Strict, modular architectural containment using asymmetric grid groupings (2x2, 2x1, 1x1, 1x2). Every product, verification metric, and operational parameter occupies a definitive geometric coordinate.
- **Spatial Restraint**: Soft, optical layer separation replacing heavy elevation. The canvas prioritizes atmospheric depth via tinted atmospheric glass, crisp hairline boundaries, and translucent floating pills that anchor contextual metadata.
- **Organic Precision**: The color balance contrasts clinical utilitarian black (#020402, #1F241F) against an ethereal gamut of mineral greens and forest mists (#F7F9F6 through #C5EFCB), evoking quiet luxury, verified provenance, and sustainable circular custody.

## Colors

The palette references botanical mineral tones and forest canopies, anchored by deep carbon shades that establish crisp commercial legibility.

### Surface and Canvas Distribution
- **App Canvas (Primary Background)**: `#F7F9F6` provides an ultra-light, desaturated mist baseline that avoids harsh clinical white while maintaining high contrast against photography.
- **Nested Bento Surface / Secondary Layer**: `#F0F4EF` defines secondary bento tiles, recessed filter shelves, and subtle modular containers.
- **Dark Structural Blocks**: `#1F241F` (Forest Black) forms hero sections, global navigation headers, deep checkout drawers, and editorial contrast panels. `#020402` (Near Black) is reserved strictly for high-priority typography, icon glyphs, and micro-dividers on dark themes.

### Accents & Botanical Scale
- **Primary Action & Structural Midtones**: `#647A67` (Primary Forest) guides active navigation markers, toggles, and verified badges. Supported by `#3C433B` (Forest Gray) for secondary text and tertiary borders.
- **Supporting Foliage Spectrum**: `#758173` (Muted Sage), `#8FA38A` (Soft Sage), `#A9C5A0` (Light Sage), `#B8D2B3` (Pale Forest), and `#C6DEC6` (Very Light Sage) form the harmonic ladder for availability indicators, risk tier status chips, calendar rental timelines, and thin structural strokes.
- **Luminous Accent**: `#C5EFCB` (Soft Mint) serves as the primary visual punctuation—used sparingly for primary interactive micro-states, live deposit return indicators, verified tag glows, and hero callout highlights.

## Typography

The typography couples the architectural geometric warmth of **Manrope** for display and section headers with the surgical, technical clarity of **Geist** for listings, data tables, micro-labels, and interactive elements.

- **Numerics & Financial Legibility**: All pricing, date range intervals, security deposit schedules, and insurance calculations enforce CSS `font-feature-settings: "tnum" 1` (tabular figures). This guarantees columnar alignment across rental ledgers and daily rate comparison tables.
- **Optical Tracking**: Large-scale headlines enforce negative letter-spacing (`-0.025em` down to `-0.01em`) to tighten optical flow and reinforce an editorial luxury feel. Micro-labels, metadata markers, and status indicators switch to uppercase tracking (`+0.08em`) to guarantee legibility at small scale.
- **Hierarchy Rules**: Editorial storytelling leads with Manrope Medium/SemiBold. Direct component text, spec tables, verification breakdowns, and inputs remain strictly within Geist Regular and Medium.

## Layout & Spacing

The structural layout uses a modular, multi-tier bento system organized across an 8pt base grid rhythm:

- **Desktop (1200px+)**: 12-column layout with 28px (`1.75rem`) gutters and 56px (`3.5rem`) outer canvas margins. Standard bento aspect cells scale across:
  - `2x2` (Hero curated product showcases & interactive 3D inspection windows)
  - `2x1` (Rental duration scheduling, pickup map integration)
  - `1x1` (Insurance specifications, owner credentials, condition audit ratings)
  - `1x2` (Vertical sticky summary cards, deposit-to-credit conversion ledgers)
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px)**: 8-column layout with 20px (`1.25rem`) gutters and 32px (`2rem`) margins. The bento grid reflows: 2-column wide elements take 4 columns, preserving relative spatial balance.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column layout with 16px gutters and 16px outer margins. Bento grids flatten into vertically stacked tactile cards with full-width horizontal scroll rails for secondary metadata widgets.
- **Rhythm Rules**: Bento modules use internal content padding mapped directly to `space-lg` (24px) for small blocks and `space-xl` (40px) for prominent hero panels. Micro components inside bento tiles follow `space-xs` (4px) and `space-sm` (8px) internal gaps.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses physical transparency, crisp hair-thin structural boundaries, and subtle environmental ambient diffusion rather than standard drop shadows.

- **Hairline Boundaries**: Bento panels and spatial cards use a 1px solid or semi-transparent stroke: `rgba(100, 122, 103, 0.18)` on light containers and `rgba(198, 222, 198, 0.12)` against Forest Black surfaces.
- **Spatial Floating Pills**: Floating contextual metadata (e.g., "Available This Weekend", "Verified Serial Number", "Insured up to $5,000") float above photography and bento layers via frosted glass:
  - *Light Glass Pill*: `background: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.72); backdrop-filter: blur(12px); border: 1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.40);`
  - *Dark Glass Pill*: `background: rgba(31, 36, 31, 0.82); backdrop-filter: blur(12px); border: 1px solid rgba(198, 222, 198, 0.18);`
- **Ambient Forest Diffusion**: When modals or contextual drawers lift from the canvas, elevation is achieved with an ultra-soft botanical shadow: `box-shadow: 0 16px 40px -12px rgba(31, 36, 31, 0.08), 0 2px 6px 0 rgba(31, 36, 31, 0.03)`.

## Shapes

The geometric framework balances clean architectural discipline with smooth spatial ergonomics.

- **Core Bento Modules**: Standard cards and inner functional widgets use an explicit corner radius of `12px` to `14px` (`rounded-lg`), ensuring nested separation without visual noise.
- **Macro Hero Enclosures**: Primary hero panels, major category displays, and high-impact media containers scale up to `18px` to `20px` (`rounded-xl`).
- **Interactive Controls & System Pills**: Primary CTAs ("LIST AN ITEM"), quick-filter chips, user identity avatars, and status badges use pill geometry (`border-radius: 9999px`). 
- **Nested Proportionality**: Inner interactive modules contained within a `14px` bento block maintain an inner radius of `8px` (`rounded-md`), preserving consistent equidistant visual borders.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Controls
- **Primary CTA ("LIST AN ITEM" / "RESERVE DATES")**: Full pill styling (`border-radius: 9999px`). Forest Black (`#1F241F`) solid fill with high-contrast Soft Mint text (`#C5EFCB`) or pure white typography. Hover elevates subtly with an inner border glow of `#C5EFCB` at 30% opacity.
- **Secondary Action**: Translucent mist fill (`#F0F4EF`) with a hairline border in `#C6DEC6`, typography set in `#1F241F`. Active states deepen to `#647A67` with white text.
- **Ghost Action**: Transparent background with a `1px` border of `rgba(100, 122, 103, 0.25)`, typography set in `#3C433B`.

### Spatial Bento Cards & Product Units
- **Product Listing Tile**: Crisp 1px `#C6DEC6` border over `#FFFFFF` or `#F0F4EF` base. Media section features an uncropped aspect ratio with the primary day-rate locked in the bottom-left corner in bold tabular figures. Floating glass pill in top-right displays condition tier (e.g., "Pristine", "Studio Verified").
- **Verification Badges & Spec Pills**: Ultra-compact (height: 24px), pill-shaped containers featuring a 6px circular indicator dot (pulsing `#C5EFCB` for active/available, `#758173` for reserved). Text uses `label-caps` in Geist.

### Form Fields & Rental Selectors
- **Date Range & Spec Inputs**: Enclosed within `#F7F9F6` containers with a 1px border of `rgba(100, 122, 103, 0.2)`. Focus states remove default browser outlines, replacing them with a crisp 1.5px border of `#647A67` and a soft ambient halo of `rgba(197, 239, 203, 0.4)`. Floating labels sit inside the field using `label-floating`.

### Checkboxes, Radios, and Toggles
- **Toggles (Instant Book, Insurance Tier)**: Pill track in `#C6DEC6`. Active thumb shifts smoothly into Forest Black (`#1F241F`) with a central active indicator in Soft Mint (`#C5EFCB`).
- **Checkboxes**: 18px rounded squares (`roundedness: 4px`) with a 1.5px stroke of `#758173`. Checked state transitions to `#1F241F` with a custom crisp geometric checkmark in `#C5EFCB`.

### Rental-Specific Extensions
- **Deposit & Value Ledger**: A micro-bento component illustrating total product value, temporary deposit hold, and base rental cost. Split into dynamic segmented bars utilizing the foliage ladder (`#647A67`, `#A9C5A0`, `#C5EFCB`) to communicate low-friction trust and security.
- **Item Condition Timeline**: Multi-step vertical or horizontal connector tracking pre-rental inspection photos, check-in timestamps, and return verification stamps with high-contrast tabular timestamps.
